03 / Engineering Blueprint

End-to-End Pipeline Architecture.

A multi-stage asynchronous vision graph operating seamlessly from ingestion to final JSON/CSV timeline metadata dispatch.

01

Perceptual Plane Segmentation

Applies Canny edge filtering, morphological closing, and Hough line transforms to identify candidate geometric planar boundaries across wall, vehicle, and billboard regions.

• Sub-pixel contour snapping
• Perspective vanishing lines
02

3D Homography & Quad Sorting

Orders polygon vertices cyclically (Top-Left, Top-Right, Bottom-Right, Bottom-Left) and computes the 8-DOF Direct Linear Transform (DLT) homography matrix $\mathbf{H}$.

• SVD nullspace decomposition
• Coplanarity validation
03

Kalman Temporal State Filter

Tracks continuous quad states across sequential frames using an 8-state Kalman Filter $[\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{\dot{x}}]$ with Hungarian IoU data association to avoid coordinate jitter.

• Occlusion state propagation
• Sub-millisecond tracking step
04

Timeline Slot Partitioning

Groups frames containing identical continuous surfaces into consolidated non-overlapping advertisement slots. Eliminates duplicate cards and outputs clean timecodes.

• Consolidated timestamp intervals
• Formatted CSV & JSON exports
04 / Mathematical Foundation

ScoreEngine Objective Function.

How every candidate surface is rigorously scored using a weighted multi-factor objective function balancing area, orthogonality, stability, and occlusion penalties.

Consolidated Scoring Formula
Sfinal = wa·Sarea + wo·Sortho + ws·Sstab + wc·Scont - λocc·Pocc

Where default production hyperparameters are normalized: wa = 0.30, wo = 0.35, ws = 0.20, wc = 0.15, and λocc = 0.50.

1. Surface Area
Weight: 30%

Ratio of candidate polygon pixels to total image resolution, penalizing microscopic or off-screen spaces.

2. Orthogonality
Weight: 35%

Angular deviation of opposite quad edges from 90° right angles, ensuring viewer readability.

3. Temporal Stability
Weight: 20%

Inverse coordinate variance $\sigma^2$ across frames, rewarding smooth trajectories over erratic shaking.

4. Edge Contrast
Weight: 15%

Luminance difference between interior planar pixels and ambient exterior background for maximum pop.

5. Occlusion Penalty
Deduction: -0.5x

Dynamic IoU intersection with foreground pedestrians, vehicle pillars, handrails, or windows.
